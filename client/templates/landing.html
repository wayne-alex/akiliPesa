How it works

Three steps, no ambiguity

Stake & get matched

Deposit by M-Pesa and join the next scheduled room. If fewer than 2 players are present at kickoff, your stake is returned to your wallet automatically — no round runs with only one player.

Answer under the clock

Each of the 10 questions carries a fixed answer window. Points are awarded for correctness first, then reduced the longer you take to respond — so recall and reflex both matter.

Instant, transparent payout

The room closes, the leaderboard locks, and winnings are released straight to your wallet. The pot breakdown — including our share — is shown to every player before the room starts.

Room formats

Play alone, or bring your squad

Every room is scheduled in advance, so you always know when it starts and what it costs to enter.

Solo room

Enter for KSh 50 or KSh 100. You compete individually against everyone else in that room.

Chama room (teams of 4)

Four players pool KSh 200. The team's answer on each question is set by majority vote among the four; if the vote ties, the fastest response wins the tie.

Fair play & compliance

Built to be checked, not just trusted

Licensed operation

AkiliPesa operates under licensing from Kenya's gambling regulator. Our licence number and status are verifiable and posted here before public launch.

Responsible play

Set a daily or weekly deposit limit, take a break with self-exclusion, and see your running stake history at any time in your wallet.

Escrowed stakes

Stakes are held in a segregated account until a room settles. If a room doesn't reach minimum players, funds return automatically — no manual request needed.
